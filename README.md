
# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce sales data to identify **sales trends, revenue performance, top-performing product categories, and country-wise sales performance**.

The analysis was performed to understand overall business performance and identify key patterns in sales and revenue.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze sales trends over time.
* Understand overall revenue performance.
* Identify the top-performing product categories.
* Compare sales performance across different countries.
* Identify important patterns and trends in the e-commerce business.

---

## 🗂️ Dataset

The dataset contains e-commerce sales information including details related to:

* Orders
* Products
* Categories
* Sales
* Revenue
* Countries
* Order dates

The dataset was used to analyze sales performance across different time periods, categories, and countries.

| **Power BI**        | Data visualization and dashboard creation |

---

## 🔎 Analysis Performed

### 📈 Sales Trend Analysis

Analyzed sales performance over time to identify:

* Increasing or decreasing sales trends.
* Changes in sales across different time periods.
* Periods with higher and lower sales.

### 💰 Revenue Analysis

Analyzed overall revenue to understand:

* Revenue performance over time.
* Periods contributing the highest revenue.
* Overall revenue trends.

### 🏆 Top Categories

Analyzed product categories to identify:

* Highest-performing categories.
* Categories contributing significantly to sales.
* Differences in sales performance between categories.

### 🌍 Country-wise Analysis

Compared sales across countries to identify:

* Countries generating the highest sales.
* Countries with comparatively lower sales.
* Geographical patterns in e-commerce performance.

---

## 📊 Dashboard

An interactive dashboard was created to visualize the analysis, including:

* **Sales Trends**
* **Revenue**
* **Top Categories**
* **Country-wise Sales**
* Key business performance indicators

The dashboard allows users to easily understand the overall sales performance and identify important business trends.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis provides insights into:

* How sales changed over time.
* How revenue performed across different periods.
* Which product categories performed the best.
* Which countries contributed the most to sales.
* Key areas of strong and weak sales performance.

This project demonstrates practical experience in **e-commerce data analysis, SQL querying, Excel analysis, and Power BI visualization**.
